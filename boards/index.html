PRESS TURN ICON DYNAMICS

In classical Megami Tensei mechanics, turn count is not a static allotment. It is an elastic pool governed by elemental foresight.

FULL TURN ICON

Default unit resource. Consuming a full icon while hitting a normal attack burns the entire token. Hitting a weakness preserves it as a flashing Half Icon.

HALF TURN ICON

The reward of strategic precision. Acts as a complete turn for any party unit, but cannot be further subdivided.

NULL & MISS PENALTY

A catastrophic error in tactical judgment. Striking an immune or absorbed element immediately consumes 2 complete icons, ending the turn.
